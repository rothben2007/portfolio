About me

I am a second-year Aerospace Engineering student and student pilot at Texas A&M University. My interests center on aircraft design, aerodynamics, flight dynamics, aviation maintenance, and the process of turning engineering analysis into hardware that can be built and tested.

Through aircraft design teams, personal RC aircraft projects, aviation maintenance, CAD, rapid prototyping, and flight testing, I have developed a hands-on approach to solving engineering problems. I am seeking opportunities to contribute these skills to aerospace design teams and internships while continuing to grow as an engineer and pilot.

What I'm doing

  • Aircraft Design & Aerodynamics

    Using XFLR5 and engineering trade studies to evaluate aircraft performance, stability, control authority, and aerodynamic configuration choices.

  • CAD & Rapid Prototyping

    Designing components in Autodesk Inventor and Fusion 360, then producing and iterating parts through 3D printing, woodworking, and hands-on fabrication.

  • Aviation & Aircraft Maintenance

    Building practical experience through flight training and hands-on airframe and powerplant maintenance, including work on Beechcraft Model 18 aircraft and Pratt & Whitney R-985 engines.
