About me

I am a second-year Aerospace Engineering student at Texas A&M University and a student pilot focused on aircraft design, aerodynamics, flight dynamics, and hands-on engineering. My experience ranges from stability and control analysis for Texas A&M's AIAA Design/Build/Fly team to maintaining Beechcraft Model 18 aircraft and Pratt & Whitney R-985 radial engines. I enjoy working where analysis, design, fabrication, testing, and real-world aircraft operations come together.

I build projects to understand how engineering decisions perform outside of a CAD model. That has included designing and flight-testing a powered RC glider, developing Python tools for aircraft performance trade studies, creating components in Autodesk Inventor and Fusion 360, and designing an underwater competition ROV. I am building this portfolio for both aerospace internship recruiters and engineering design teams, with an emphasis on practical problem solving, collaboration, and turning ideas into hardware that can be tested.
