About me

Mechanical Engineer at Texas A&M University (3.84 GPA) interested in rocket propulsion, precision fabrication, and embedded systems. Currently an Engineering Intern at Albireo LLC, where I design control-system electronics — KiCad schematics and CNC-milled PCBs — for a 10-ft motorized telescope that automatically slews to any star or constellation, including the power distribution board that runs the platform off a single 12 V battery. I also serve as Chief Engineer of the Elysium II liquid rocket engine on the Texas A&M Rocket Engine Design Team (RED), directing every engine subteam and giving the final technical checkoff before a test is run.

Contributing to the College Station community through dedicated service and large scale fundraising. As a member of the Aggie Club of Engineers Service Team, I have helped raise over $5,000 for Camp Hope (PTSD Foundation of America) and participate in the Adopt-A-Street initiative to maintain local infrastructure. This experience reinforces my commitment to teamwork and civic responsibility alongside my technical studies.

What I'm doing

  • Machining & Fabrication

    Learning fabrication and design skills through my work with the Texas A&M RED. I have experience with CAD software, CNC machining, and 3D printing.

  • Embedded Systems & Programming

    Developing embedded systems and microcontroller programming skills through my work with Turtle Robotics. I have experience with Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and C/C++ programming through my organization wide competition, finishing 3rd out of 34 teams.
